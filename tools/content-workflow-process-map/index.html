Content Workflow / Process Map

A content workflow maps every stage in the production pipeline, the role responsible at each stage, the criteria for moving to the next, and any parallel review tracks (legal, brand, subject-matter expert). Making the workflow explicit surfaces where work actually stalls, usually review queues and approval gates, and gives everyone a shared vocabulary for discussing bottlenecks.

The defined stages a piece passes through from idea to published.

Plan / Brief → Create / Review / Publish / Distribute → Track KPIs ↺

When to use this

  • When content gets stuck and no one can explain where, the piece is 'in review' with no accountable owner.
  • To clarify role distinctions between writers, editors, subject matter experts, and approvers.
  • When scaling a content team or adding external contributors who lack institutional knowledge of the process.
