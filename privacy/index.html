Privacy & data

This site is a free, unmonetized reference. There are no ads, and your data is never sold or shared. Apart from anything you choose to send through a form or the survey, the only visitor data collected is anonymous usage analytics, and only if you consent.

Who runs this site

The Content Strategy Library is run by Tommy Stubblefield. To exercise any of the rights below, or ask anything about your data, please use the contact form, no email address is published here to keep spam down.

What is collected, and when

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the library is used. Until you accept, Google Analytics runs in a cookieless mode that sets no cookie and stores nothing on your device. If you click Reject, it stays that way permanently.

When enabled, Google Analytics may process: pages you view, approximate location (country/region), your device and browser type, and the site that referred you. Google Analytics 4 does not store your full IP address. The site sets no advertising cookies and does no cross-site tracking.

Legal basis

For visitors in the EU/UK and similar regions, the legal basis for analytics cookies is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (see below). Essential, first-party functionality, remembering your cookie choice and anything you save in the Workspace, is stored locally in your browser, is not tracking, and is never sent to us. Survey answers, and any email address you choose to give us, are processed on the basis of your consent.

Cookies used

  • _ga, _ga_* (Google Analytics), distinguish anonymous visitors and sessions. Set only after you Accept; last up to ~13 months.
  • Local storage (first-party, functional), your cookie choice and Workspace items. Never leaves your browser.

Who your data is shared with

Analytics data is processed by Google as a data processor on our behalf. Google may process it in the United States under its standard data-transfer safeguards (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses). Data is retained according to the Google Analytics retention setting for this property.

Survey responses are collected by Tally, a form service based in Belgium that encrypts responses and stores them in Europe.

The forms on this site

If you use the contact form or the “Submit a Tool” form, what you type (including any email you provide so we can reply) is sent to the site owner’s inbox via Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. It is used only to respond to you and is not added to any marketing list.

The practitioner survey

The practitioner survey is optional and does not ask for your name. Results are reported only in aggregate, as totals and percentages. Individual answers are never published, and no group small enough to identify someone is reported on its own.

At the end you can choose to leave your email address. If you do, it is used once, to send you the results when they are published. You only receive news and updates if you tick that box. Responses are kept only as long as needed to analyze and publish the results. If you left an email and want your response deleted, use the contact form.

Email

This site follows the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act and similar rules elsewhere. You only get email from us if you have expressly asked for it, for example by ticking the box for news and updates or by asking for survey results. Sending a message through a form never adds you to a list, and your email address is never sold, rented, or traded.

Every email we send says clearly who it is from, has an honest subject line, includes a postal address, and has a working unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe at any time, from any email, by clicking that link or by replying with the word “unsubscribe.” Requests are honored promptly, and always within 10 business days.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data; object to or restrict processing; and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the contact form to make a request.

How to withdraw consent or opt out

  • Use the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer of any page to change your choice.
  • Clear cookies / site data in your browser settings.
  • Install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
