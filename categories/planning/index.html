Planning & Operations

12 tools in the Planning & Operations category.

  • Editorial Calendar — The dated, assigned plan that turns strategy into a publishing schedule.
  • Content Brief — The per-piece spec that gives writers what they need before they start.
  • Content Workflow / Process Map — The defined stages a piece passes through from idea to published.
  • RACI Matrix — Assigns Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed to every task.
  • Editorial Style Guide — The rulebook for grammar, punctuation, terminology, and formatting.
  • Contributor / Freelancer Guidelines — The document external writers and SMEs need to produce work that fits.
  • Idea Backlog + Prioritization (RICE/ICE) — A scored list that ensures the highest-value content gets made first.
  • Channel Strategy / Distribution Matrix — Defines which channels you publish to, and the strategic role of each.
  • The PESO Model — Integrates Paid, Earned, Shared, and Owned media into one distribution system.
  • Hero-Hub-Help — Google’s programming model: tentpole moments, regular series, always-on answers.
  • Repurposing / Atomization Matrix — Turns one pillar asset into its full ecosystem of smaller spinoffs.
  • Content Governance Plan — The long-term standards and ownership that keep content from decaying.
