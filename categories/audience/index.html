Audience

5 tools in the Audience category.

  • Audience Personas — Named composite profiles of the readers you are actually writing for.
  • Customer Journey Map — Maps what each persona needs at every stage of the experience.
  • Jobs-to-be-Done / Value Prop Canvas — Frames content around the task the reader is hiring it to complete.
  • Voice-of-Customer / Message Mining — Extracts exact customer language from reviews, calls, and support tickets.
  • See-Think-Do-Care (STDC) — Kaushik’s intent framework: plan content, offers, and KPIs by audience intent.
