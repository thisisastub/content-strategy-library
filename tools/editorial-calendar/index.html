Editorial Calendar

An editorial calendar assigns content ideas to specific dates, formats, owners, and channels. It is not a brainstorm list; it is a commitment document with accountable humans next to every row. The calendar's real value is forcing the conversation about capacity, most programs discover, when filling one out, that they have committed to more than they can produce. A realistic calendar is more useful than an aspirational one.

The dated, assigned plan that turns strategy into a publishing schedule.

Topic / Format / Owner / Date

When to use this

  • When publishing is reactive, ideas are executed as they arise rather than planned for coverage and balance.
  • When social content managers "feel like they need a post for this week" or leaders ask "what are we doing for XYZ holiday."
  • To coordinate content releases with product launches, campaigns, seasonal moments, and external events.
