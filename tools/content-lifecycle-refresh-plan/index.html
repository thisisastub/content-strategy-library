Content Lifecycle + Refresh Plan

A content lifecycle plan defines the phases a piece moves through after publication: active (performing), monitored (watched for decay), flagged for refresh (traffic or accuracy declining), refreshed (updated), or retired (removed or redirected). Without a lifecycle plan, content ages silently. With one, review triggers are defined in advance and the decision between refresh and retire becomes deliberate rather than a backlog of overdue maintenance.

Sets audit cadence, update triggers, and a sunset policy for aging content.

Create → Publish → Monitor → Refresh → Retire

When to use this

  • When a content audit reveals a large proportion of the catalog is outdated, underperforming, or actively misleading.
  • When establishing a governance model and needing operational procedures to accompany the ownership decisions.
  • When organic search performance for existing content is declining and a systematic refresh program is needed.
