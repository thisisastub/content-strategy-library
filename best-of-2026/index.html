Best of 2026

The resources, tools, and people that moved content strategy forward this year. Honorees are announced on this page.

Content strategy in practice

  • Framework of the year, A model or method that changed how teams plan, make, or measure content.
  • Book, A book that practitioners kept reaching for this year.
  • Newsletter, A regular read that made the practice sharper.
  • Podcast, A show worth the commute for anyone working in content.
  • Course or training, Teaching that turned people into better strategists.
  • Template or toolkit, A free resource that saved teams real time.
  • Content team, A team whose program set an example for everyone else.

Content strategy influencers

  • Lifetime contribution, Someone whose work over many years shaped how the field thinks and works.
  • Educator, Someone who taught content strategy to more people this year, through courses, books, or talks.
  • Rising voice, A newer voice whose ideas are already changing the conversation.
  • Bridge builder, Someone who brought content strategy into UX, product, marketing, or the executive suite.
  • Community builder, Someone who created spaces where practitioners learn from each other.
